# One-tone carousel

A reader swipes fast. If one slide says something bad and something good at once, they lose the thread.
Give every slide **one tone**, and tell the story in order.

## The order

| Slide | Tone | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | curious | the rule or the surprise, in under 12 words |
| 2. Proof | neutral | the real screen, the real number, where it came from |
| 3. Mistake | bad | what went wrong, or what most people do |
| 4. Cost | bad | what it cost, in a number with a unit |
| 5. Fix | good | the turn: what we changed, on its own slide |
| 6. Payoff | good | the result, and the one thing to do now |

Short stories can merge 3 and 4, or drop 2. **Never put the problem and its fix on the same slide or in the same
sentence.** The turn always gets its own slide.

## Rules that bind every slide

- **Real screenshots only for your own product, app or software.** The real screen is the proof. An AI drawing of your
  own app reads as fake, and people notice.
- **No dense text made by an image model.** Image models misspell. Put text on slides with a real layout tool
  (HTML, Figma, Canva), never baked into a generated picture.
- **One idea per slide, under 25 words of body text.** Text at least 28 px on a 1080 px slide.
- **Numbers carry units** (`$40`, `3 days`, `12 bikes`). A claim with no number is a weaker claim.
- **No price on slide 1.** The hook earns the swipe; a price stops it.
- **Never promise what is not live.**

## Decide the tap path before you write

Every post needs one thing the reader does next, decided first:
- "Comment WORD and we send you the link" (needs a comment-to-DM tool switched on), or
- "Link in bio", landing on the exact page for this post, never a homepage, or
- a story the same day with a link sticker.

## The caption

- Line 1 repeats the hook in other words (it shows above "more").
- 3 to 5 short lines, the tap path on the last line.
- English and Spanish: two blocks, same meaning, Spanish first if most readers are Spanish speakers.
- 3 to 5 hashtags, specific, at the end.

## What to produce

1. The slide list: number, tone, headline, body, and the exact screenshot each one needs (which screen, which state).
2. The caption, EN and ES.
3. The tap path, and what must be switched on for it to work.
4. A check: read the slides in order and confirm no slide carries two tones.
