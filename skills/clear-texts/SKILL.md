# Clear texts

A text is read on a phone, often while the reader is doing something else. A wall of text gets skipped.
Every text you draft follows this shape.

## The shape

1. **One headline line, under 8 words,** that says what happened. Example: `Four new things in your bookings today.`
2. **A blank line between every item.** WhatsApp and SMS keep blank lines. Use them.
3. **One item = a bold label, a colon, then one or two short sentences.** WhatsApp bold is `*text*`. The label is the
   word the reader looks for (`*Deposit:*`), not a sentence.
4. **Say where to tap in the reader's own words:** `open the booking, then Deposit`. No arrows, slashes or
   parentheses. No quotes around button names.
5. **At most 5 items per text.** More than 5 is two texts, or a link to a page.
6. **The last line is the one thing to do,** as a short ask. Example: `Try it with the next guest and tell me how it goes.`
7. **Under 900 characters.** If it does not fit, cut items, not words.
8. **No emoji in the body.** One emoji at the start of the headline is fine when the news is good.
9. **Write in the reader's language.** Spanish to a Spanish reader, same shape.
10. **Never promise a date or a feature that is not live.** "Coming" means someone is building it. "On" means you checked it works.

## Template

```
*Headline in under eight words*

*Label:* what it does. Where to find it, in words.

*Label:* what it does. Where to find it, in words.

The one thing to do next.
```

## Example

Before:

> Hi! So just wanted to let you know we updated a few things, the deposit is now taken when they book (you can see it
> in the booking screen -> Deposit tab), also the calendar shows the weather now and we fixed the bug where the
> return time showed wrong, oh and the price for 2 days changed to $90. Let me know what you think!!

After:

```
*Four changes in your bookings*

*Deposit:* we take it when the guest books. Open the booking, then Deposit.

*Weather:* the calendar shows it for each day.

*Return time:* fixed, it shows the right time now.

*Two-day price:* now $90.

Look at tomorrow's bookings and tell me if anything looks wrong.
```

## Before you hand it over

- [ ] Headline under 8 words
- [ ] A blank line between items
- [ ] 5 items or fewer, each with a bold label
- [ ] Tap paths in words, no arrows
- [ ] Ends with one ask
- [ ] Under 900 characters
- [ ] Nothing promised that is not live
- [ ] Every point in the original is still there
